# Data sourcing and copyright policy

## Reading orders

Prefer public reading-order pages that permit linking and factual extraction, community lists with clear attribution/licensing, or manually curated lists with source citations and version dates.

For every guide, record:
- source title and URL;
- access/review date;
- ordering rationale (publication, chronological, or recommended);
- editorial changes and known omissions.

Do not present a short sample as a complete or canonical order. Flag disputed or optional tie-in placement.

## Metadata

Use public metadata APIs only under their current terms, attribution requirements, API-key rules, and rate limits. Marvel's public developer API may provide issue metadata subject to those conditions. DC metadata availability is more fragmented and may require licensed/public sources or manual curation.

## Marvel Unlimited

Do not scrape authenticated pages, bypass access controls, defeat DRM, or redistribute Marvel Unlimited guide text/artwork. Use an authorized API/export only if Marvel documents and permits it.

## Artwork

Do not bundle copyrighted covers or comic pages without permission. Store metadata and links; embed images only where the source terms permit it. A public URL is not automatically permission to redistribute an image.
